My name is Kalyn Beach. I've been building software since 2010 and working professionally since 2015, moving across product architecture, systems design, interface craft, and delivery.

I like work where engineering rigor meets expressive software: taking ambiguous, technically demanding problems and reducing them to systems that feel clear, dependable, and alive.

Most recently, I led engineering on Gist GEO, an agentic AI product at ProRata AI, taking it from initial architecture through its alpha with first customers while helping a young team adopt disciplined engineering practices. Before that, I spent nine years across Demand Media, Leaf Group, and World of Good Brands scaling high-traffic publishing platforms, leading platform modernizations and production data migrations, building internal web-performance tooling, and shipping browser-based music apps.

Outside of work, I make [music](/sound) and build my own instruments and infrastructure: a portable [agent workflow system](/software/agents), a [real-time audio engine](/software/kkb-audio), and the design system powering this site.
