## Kevelle

Kevelle is my middle name and the name of my design system. I design its color, type, surfaces and motion in Paper. I took its look from hardware labels, hairline grids, dithered meters and oscilloscope traces.

The registry at [registry.kkb.systems](https://registry.kkb.systems) has 91 items you can install with the shadcn CLI. This site installs its components and tokens from there.

## kalynbeach.net

This is my personal site, and it plays my music. Home's waveform plays from the keyboard, and the mini player follows you from page to page. I upload tracks from a dashboard, and each track can have its own saved waveform look.

It's built with Next.js 16, React 19, Convex and Clerk, and the code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/kalynbeach/kalynbeach-net). Claude Code and Codex built the November 2026 redesign as stacked PRs, each reviewed by a second model.
