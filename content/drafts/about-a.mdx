## Who I am

I'm Kalyn Kevelle Beach, also known as kevelle and KKB. I'm a musician, artist and engineer in Los Angeles.

My interests include science, sound, music, art, history, systems thinking, software architecture and interface design. I'm also interested in AI and agents.

## What I do

I'm a senior product engineer. I've built for the web since 2015, most recently in TypeScript and React.

My most recent role was lead engineer on Gist GEO, an agentic AI product at ProRata AI. Before that I spent nine years on high-traffic content sites at Demand Media, Leaf Group and World of Good Brands.

On my own I work in `kkb`, my monorepo for agents, audio experiments and the Kevelle design system. I also build kkb-audio, a real-time audio engine in Rust that runs in the browser and on macOS.

## How I got here

I started programming around 2012 and took computer science courses at Chapman University. After that I kept teaching myself while running shifts at Starbucks. In 2015 I got my first contract web job. I started building with AI in 2023, first a chat tool for music makers and later MCP servers for music and audio. Now Claude Code, Codex and Pi share one set of my instructions and skills. A model from a second family reviews what they write.

The full timeline and my projects are on the [Software](/software) page.
