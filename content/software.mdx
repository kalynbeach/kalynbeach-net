## Kevelle

My personal design system, developed in my `kkb` monorepo and distributed as a shadcn registry at [registry.kkb.systems](https://registry.kkb.systems). It packages tokens, typography, typeset rules, accessible primitives, automated contrast and drift checks, and audio-visual blocks including the Phosphor WebGPU oscilloscope (with a WebGL2 fallback) and WavePlayer.

Kevelle powers this site, my interface studies in [ui-lab](https://github.com/kalynbeach/ui-lab), and a suite of browser audio tools in `kkb` spanning an FFT spectrogram, an eight-voice AudioWorklet synth with Web MIDI, binaural beats, and an Ableton Live `.als` set viewer.

## kalynbeach.net

This site, rebuilt in late 2026 on Kevelle. Public pages are authored in MDX and prerendered with draft and production gating, while the persistent waveform stage and music player stay live across route transitions and stream track and look updates from a private admin dashboard. The source is open at [github.com/kalynbeach/kalynbeach-net](https://github.com/kalynbeach/kalynbeach-net).
